Replayfy AI is a built-in analyst that lives inside your dashboard. Ask it a
question in plain language and it plans an investigation, reads your real
session and analytics data, and answers with the evidence to back it up. It can
also take work off your plate — build a Funnel or Cohort, watch an issue, or
draft an alert — and it proactively surfaces what changed on your Overview
before you think to ask.

Every answer is grounded in your **structured session data** — events,
funnels, releases, crashes, journeys — never in a black box, and never guessed
from raw video. When an answer depends on a time range, Replayfy AI tells you
which window it used.

<Info>
  Replayfy AI uses **leading language models** under the hood to plan and
  narrate. The reasoning is grounded in your workspace's own data — the model
  never sees another customer's sessions, and it only reports numbers it
  actually pulled.
</Info>

## Where to find it

Replayfy AI is reachable from every page of the dashboard at
[app.replayfy.app](https://app.replayfy.app):

- **The Ask AI panel** — a floating <Icon icon="sparkles" /> **Ask AI** button
  in the bottom-right corner. Click it and a chat panel springs open. It stays
  with you as you move between Recordings, Funnels, Users, and Settings, so you
  can ask about whatever you're looking at.
- **The Ask bar on the Overview** — a command box at the top of your
  [Overview](/products/product-analytics) with rotating example questions. Start typing
  and press <kbd>⏎</kbd> to ask.
- **"Investigate" actions** — buttons scattered through the product (on a
  signal, a crash, a drop-off) open the panel pre-loaded with the right
  question.

## How to ask

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask AI">
    Click the <Icon icon="sparkles" /> **Ask AI** button in the bottom-right of
    any page, or use the Ask bar at the top of your Overview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask in plain language">
    Type a real question — no query syntax, no filters to configure. Press
    <kbd>⏎</kbd> to send. You can pick one of the suggested prompts to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it investigate">
    Replayfy AI shows its plan and streams each step as it runs —
    _"Investigating conversion…"_, _"Reading release 1.6.2…"_ — then writes the
    answer token by token. Expand the **thinking** row to see exactly which
    sources it read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the evidence">
    Answers come with citation chips. Click a session chip to jump straight into
    that recording, or a funnel chip to open the funnel. Use the suggested
    follow-up buttons to keep digging.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Good questions are specific and outcome-shaped. Try **"Why did checkout
  conversion drop this week?"**, **"What's hurting stability right now?"**,
  **"Compare iOS vs Android retention"**, or **"Which cohort recovered
  fastest?"**
</Tip>

## Grounded answers, not guesses

Replayfy AI is deliberately honest about what it knows:

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **The time window** | When an answer depends on a range, the reply states it — _"in the last 7 days"_, _"across the 1.6.2 release"_ — so you never mistake a narrow window for the whole picture. |
| **Citations** | Evidence chips link to the exact sessions, funnels, console lines, or crashes the answer rests on. |
| **Confidence** | Answers can carry a high / medium / low confidence flag when the underlying sample is thin. |
| **Honest empty states** | "No one reached this step" and "there's no data in this window" are reported as different things — the AI won't invent a number to fill a gap. |

<Note>
  Answers are grounded in your **structured** product data — tracked events,
  funnel steps, releases, crash groups, journeys and session metadata — not in
  the pixels of a replay. The richer your event tracking, the sharper the
  answers. See [Track events](/guides/track-events).
</Note>

## Actions it can take for you

Beyond answering, Replayfy AI can do the work. Ask it to build something and it
assembles the definition, shows you a preview, and creates it — stamped
**"Created with Replayfy AI"** so your team can see where it came from.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Build a Funnel" icon="filter">

    creates the funnel for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create a Cohort" icon="users">
    "Make a cohort of users who rage-tapped checkout on Android" — it translates
    the description into a real, saved cohort.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Watch an issue" icon="bell">
    "Alert me if crash-free rate drops below 99%" — it drafts the alert and can
    watch a specific issue for recurrence.

### The confirmation gate

Anything that writes outside Replayfy — filing an issue, posting to a channel —
**pauses for your approval first**. Replayfy AI shows you the full outgoing
payload (the issue body, the exact Slack fields) and nothing is sent until you
click **Confirm**.

<Warning>
  Read-only questions run freely. Actions that create, update, delete, or send
  are previewed and require an explicit **Confirm** — Replayfy AI never writes
  or sends on your behalf without it.
</Warning>

### It learns your workspace

When a question is ambiguous — _"which checkout event do you mean?"_ — Replayfy
AI asks, and remembers your answer so it never has to ask again. Admins can
review, correct, or forget everything the AI has learned about the workspace,
and every action it takes is written to an **audit trail** you can inspect.

## Proactive intelligence on the Overview

You don't have to ask first. When AI is on, your [Overview](/products/product-analytics)
leads with insights the AI generated by reading the same data you'd query:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Storyline" icon="sparkles">
    An analyst-written lead at the top of the Overview — the single most
    important thing happening in your product right now, in prose, with a
    confidence chip and the supporting evidence. It's regenerated as your data
    moves, never templated.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Signals" icon="chart-line">
    The ledger of what moved — conversion, stability, engagement and segment
    shifts, ranked by impact. With AI on, each signal gains a short analyst read
    on top of the raw numbers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Worth watching & experience health" icon="shield-halved">
    A rolled-up health read of the current window and a short watchlist of the
    things most likely to need your attention next — surfaced before they become
    incidents.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Prefer a purely deterministic dashboard? Toggle Replayfy AI **off** for the
  workspace and the Overview falls back to threshold-based cards with no
  generated narrative. The switch is instant and reversible.
</Tip>

## AI credits & metering

Replayfy AI runs on **credits**. Every request — an Ask turn, a Storyline
refresh, a set of Signals — consumes credits in proportion to the work it did.
Credits are generous, round numbers rather than fractions of a cent:

- **≈ 100,000 credits = $1.** Every request is metered against this rate.
- **A monthly allowance is bundled with your plan** — from a taster on Free up
  to millions of credits on higher tiers — sized so normal usage stays inside
  it.
- **Optional top-ups.** If you run through the monthly allowance, buy more with
  a one-time **Buy credits** purchase; they stack on top of your bundled
  allowance.

You can see exactly where credits went. **Settings → Billing** shows credits
**used vs. included**, credits **remaining**, and a per-surface breakdown — how
much Ask consumed versus the proactive insights — plus a receipt for every
top-up.

<Note>
  When a workspace runs out of credits, Replayfy AI pauses rather than billing
  you by surprise — it resumes when the allowance resets or you top up. Full
  plan allowances and pricing live in
  [Workspaces & billing](/platform/workspaces-and-billing).
</Note>

## What feeds better answers

Replayfy AI reasons over whatever your SDK sends. The more you identify users
and track meaningful events, the more precise your answers about conversion,
segments, and journeys become.

<CodeGroup>
```ts npm
import { initReplay } from "@replayfyapp/browser";

const replay = initReplay({
  apiKey: "rpl_pk_YOUR_KEY",         // publishable — safe to ship
  apiHost: "https://us.replayfy.app",
});

// Tie sessions to a real user so the AI can reason about cohorts & retention
replay.identify("user_123", { plan: "pro" });

// Track the moments that matter so questions about them have data
replay.track("checkout_started", { cartValue: 84.0 });
```

```html CDN
<script src="https://cdn.replayfy.app/v1/replay.global.js"></script>
<script>
  Replayfy.init({
    apiKey: "rpl_pk_YOUR_KEY",
    apiHost: "https://us.replayfy.app",
  });
  Replayfy.identify("user_123", { plan: "pro" });
  Replayfy.track("checkout_started", { cartValue: 84.0 });
</script>
```
</CodeGroup>

<Check>
  Once events flow in, ask **"How is checkout_started converting this week?"** —
  the AI will pull the numbers, state the window, and cite the sessions behind
  them.
</Check>
