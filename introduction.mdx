Replayfy records what your users actually do — pixel-accurate session replays, taps
and clicks, screen navigation, network activity, console logs, crashes, and
performance vitals — then turns it into **funnels, cohorts, crash reports, and AI
answers** so you can see how your product is really used and fix what's broken.

Drop in one SDK per platform and you're recording in under two minutes. These docs
are the complete how-to for the whole platform — from your first session to funnels,
crash triage, alerts, and Replayfy AI.

## How it works

Each SDK captures on-device, batches efficiently, and streams to your Replayfy
ingest host. Recording, tap capture, screen detection, crash handling, and
performance monitoring all begin from a single `init` / `start` call — no manual
wiring. Settings you configure in the dashboard are fetched at startup and take
precedence over the values passed in code, so you can tune masking, sampling, or
pause recording without shipping a new build.
